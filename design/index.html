ArticleFlux — three directions

Same screen in all three: the ranked home view, with sidebar, list, and reading pane. Identical content and identical data, so the comparison is about design and nothing else. Every version keeps the real constraints — fixed-height rows, visible connection state, an explainability line on each ranked item, and the dormant-feed nudge.

01

Modern

A contemporary product. Generous spacing, soft radii, a serif reading face at a confident size, and colour used only for state. Sources get monogram tiles, ranking reasons are pills, and the article carries a reading-progress hairline. The safest to build and the easiest to live with.

Schibsted Grotesk + Newsreader light 92px rows
02

Utilitarian

A tool, not a product. System type, 34px compact rows, ruled columns with headers, a folder tree, a menubar, and a persistent key bar along the bottom. Roughly three times the items per screen, every action labelled with its key, zero ornament. Built for triaging 143 unread in four minutes.

system UI + IBM Plex Mono light, dense 34px rows
03

Fanciful

Chosen

Warm, expressive, and still honest. Plum ground, cream text, Fraunces set with its soft and wonk axes turned up. Every source owns a colour that runs through its dot, its list edge, its highlight, and a wash behind the article — so you learn your feeds by hue. Reasons are written as sentences, not stats.

Fraunces + Literata + Outfit dark, warm 96px rows resizable panes
04

Fanciful — mobile

Chosen

The same design on a phone. Three panes become three levels: the list is the home screen, sources live in a bottom sheet, and the article pushes in over the top. Actions sit in the thumb arc rather than a toolbar. Open it on a phone and it fills the screen; on desktop it renders inside a device frame you can click through.

safe-area aware 46px targets swipe back
